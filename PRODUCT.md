# DIGIPROT Product Brief

## Product

DIGIPROT presents website design and development services to business owners. Its institutional site explains the offer and provides commercial and technical WhatsApp contact paths.

## Redesign goal

The redesign should make DIGIPROT's visual and technical capability the main thing visitors notice. Contact remains available but is secondary. The user wants the full site to use substantial animation and large visual assets to express a technology capability.

## Audience

Business owners, without restrictions on business size.
